Week 7. Electronic Output Devices

Minimum Viable Product for Final Project

My goal for the MVP was ambitious—get the hardware assembled and all the motors hooked up, and maybe some elementary lines or shapes drawn. I did not achieve that, but I made meaningful progress. It took some time to familiarize and find all the ingredients for the build, from poles to t-nuts to capacitors. I spent a lot of time 3D printing because of numerous issues (printer ran out of filament, print needed to be adapted to the sized materials that we have in the lab, the printer wasn’t calibrated and printed a blob in the air, etc.). However, once that was done and I ripped the supports out, it wasn’t too difficult to assemble a lot of the rest of it. Through trial and error, I found the right sized nuts and bolts to fasten different components. Building the gantry plate took more time than I liked—I thought I had to redesign the plate when I realized I just bolted the wheels in suboptimal slots. But most everything snapped in pretty easily.
I spent a lot of time planning the back end as well. Hiteyjit helpfully suggested using Calligraphr to create text files, which might solve the input part of my handwriting pipeline. I also found repositories online (using GRBL) that have been used for pen plotters in the past. I need to do more research with the compatibility of font type files with step files/if I need to convert some file type. With Bobby’s help, I learned about making sure you keep voltages at non-fatal levels (barely wired a stepper motor without frying A4988…). This website (link) was helpful. Going to wrap up the rest of wiring and hook up pulleys once I find some zip ties.
Also, as is clear from the video, I should drill through the blue feet and attach the machine to a more stable base so it doesn't tip. I was thinking of making an inlaid printing tray sort of area that would make it easy for a user to align their target surface. THe downside of this would be that the device would be less mobile, but it's not exactly fitting in your backpack anyway.
I hope to get to elementary shapes and lines by tuesday, but I do have Eid this weekend in NY, so it feels a bit dicey.
